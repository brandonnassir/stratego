## Why the eighth failed

The vs-random point estimate is **−0.000244** — one paired unit in 1,024. It is
about as close to identical as two independently trained networks get. The bound
misses anyway, because the interval is wider than the margin it was asked to
certify:

```text
paired setup units                     1,024
sd of the paired differences           0.189374
standard error                         0.005918
95% half-width                         0.011599   <-- the instrument's resolution
predeclared margin                     0.010      <-- what it had to certify
units on which the arms differ at all  171 of 1,024
pairs needed to certify 0.010 here     1,448
```

The margin was set **16% tighter than the evaluation can resolve**. No model
quality closes that gap: the width comes from game-level noise between two
networks that are not bit-identical, and the frozen `evaluation_setup_bank_v1`
holds 1,024 pairs, not 1,448. The bootstrap is not at fault — it agrees with the
normal approximation to four decimals (1.96 × SE = 0.011599).

The contrast inside the contract makes the point sharper. **vs-init carries a
looser margin (0.03) on half the pairs and clears with room; vs-random carries a
tighter margin (0.01) on twice the pairs and cannot be certified at all.** The
tightening was justified by gate headroom, which is a different quantity from a
paired instrument's resolution.

P18-D001 said this in advance, in its own list of contradictory evidence: *the
margins are predeclared judgments; no replicate Phase 8 run exists, so there is
no measured run-to-run distribution to anchor them.* This is that gap becoming
visible.

## Separating the readings

**Observed fact.** 42/42 gates. Ten accepted metrics reproduced at delta 0.0.
Seven of eight margins cleared. The eighth has a −0.000244 point estimate and a
±0.011599 instrument against a 0.010 margin.

**Supported inference.** The accepted Phase 8 warmstart reproduces from a fresh
canonical initialization. The failing margin is a measurement-design deficit, not
a strength deficit. The margin set was not anchored on power.

**Plausible but untested.** That a replicate would land on the other side of zero
and clear the margin — its interval would be about as wide, so the outcome would
turn on the sign of a near-zero delta. That 0.189 is the characteristic
paired-difference spread for any two faithful reproductions; this is one draw.

**Not supported.** That the reproduction is inferior — nothing here shows that.
That it is *certified* non-inferior on vs-random — it is not, and the rule reads
the bound. That G1 passes.

The margin is not lowered here, and the failed gate is not converted into
telemetry. Under the frozen rule, G1 does not pass.

## The authorized replicate, and why it is not used

Section C4 conditionally authorizes exactly one full replicate to estimate MPS
run-to-run variation. It is declined, and the reason is that it does not
discriminate.

C4 exists for the case where an out-of-margin **delta** might be run-to-run noise.
Here the delta is −0.000244; there is no excursion to explain. The bound misses
on within-run game-level noise, which running the training again does not change.
A replicate would cost a further access to a sealed split the contract asks to
count and minimise, and would decide the gate on the sign of a near-zero
difference — sampling until a pass, which is precisely the drift the common
contract forbids. It remains available to the operator on request.

## Note for the reviewing chat

Re-deriving `0.011599` from the arm artifacts is the single check most worth
repeating independently: it is the difference between *the reproduction is
weaker* and *the ruler is too short*.

This is the third time the project has set a margin without checking the
instrument's resolution — after the Phase 16 shootout's 0.03 margin at 0.53 SE
and Phase 17's 120-board lane at 0.1379 MDE. A standing rule that every
predeclared margin ships with the **n** required to certify it would have caught
all three before the run rather than after.

One incidental finding the contract did not assume: the reference arm reproducing
all ten accepted metrics at exactly 0.0 shows the **greedy evaluation path is
bitwise reproducible on MPS**. Only the 25,000-step training run carries the
nondeterminism the contract warns about.
