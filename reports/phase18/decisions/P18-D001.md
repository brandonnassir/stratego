## Interpretation

**Observed fact.** All 12 Phase 8 identities recompute; the fresh C1 init rebuilds
bit-exactly; all 28,000 corpus games re-hash clean. The published code multiplies
the stored entropy prediction by `reg_norm = 10` before the residual, while the
paper's Table 19 and Eq. (1) are mutually inconsistent about that same symbol.
`force_handedness` defaults on and post-generation reflection is applied — neither
appears in the paper. The published buffer aggregates repeated outcomes per setup
by a running mean and zeroes its counters at each pool refresh. The two Phase 17
evaluation lanes correlate at only 0.238 within a candidate. The `neutral_v1`
profile samples uniformly over all 16 library families and the Phase 8 corpus
draws all three library splits.

**Supported inference.** G1 is reachable. Phase 17's setup implementation differed
from the authors' in at least five material ways, so Phase 17 does **not**
establish that the paper's setup method fails locally. Independently of that, its
evaluation was underpowered for the effect sizes at issue. No existing setup asset
can serve as an unfamiliar-opponent stratum.

**Plausible but untested.** That the entropy-unit defect dominates the Phase 17
setup result — the uncentered term is real and large, but nothing here measures
its contribution against the m = 1 outcome variance or the 32-episode batch. That
correcting all five items yields a setup policy beating the fixed library; that is
G3's question.

**Claim not supported.** That Phase 18's corrections will work — nothing was
trained. That any *specific one* of the five differences caused the Phase 17
result. That the Phase 8 control will reproduce — it was not run.
